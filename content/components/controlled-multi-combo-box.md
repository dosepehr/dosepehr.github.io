This is the [react-hook-form](https://react-hook-form.com) binding of [Multi Combo Box](/docs/components/multi-combo-box): pass the form's `control` and a field `name`, and the value, change handler and validation error are wired for you. Every other prop is forwarded to Multi Combo Box.
