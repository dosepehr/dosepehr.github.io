description: 'Dropzone wired to react-hook-form.'

This is the [react-hook-form](https://react-hook-form.com) binding of [Dropzone](/docs/components/dnd): pass the form's `control` and a field `name`, and the value, change handler and validation error are wired for you. Every other prop is forwarded to Dropzone.
